# Blind scoring instructions

Read `rubric.md`, `title-bases.json`, and `candidates.csv`. Do not read
`human-reviews.csv`. For each case, first create the rubric's evidence ledger
for every mandatory or material qualification, then set `model_hard_gate` to
`Pass` or `Reject`. Count only directly comparable functional experience; name
an evidence-based bridge before crediting adjacent work. For passing cases use
one-decimal adjustments. Keep requirement gaps separate from role shape, and
derive the company adjustment from the three internal company components. The
ranges in `README.md` are defaults, not limits: exceed them only when the
evidence warrants it, explain why, calculate the total, and provide one concise
justification.
