# Company Interest Calibration

Score each fictional company from **1–5** only for your personal interest in
its mission relatability and practical human benefit. Ignore role, pay, culture,
and likelihood of getting hired.

- **1** — no meaningful or relatable human benefit; strong preference against it
- **2** — benefit is narrow, abstract, or hard to relate to
- **3** — neutral / some concrete value, but not personally compelling
- **4** — clearly beneficial and relatable
- **5** — deeply compelling, directly aligned, or obviously improves people's lives

For each, reply with a number and a sentence explaining what makes it feel
relatable or not. Your responses will become the calibration evidence; there is
intentionally no answer key.

## Calibration interpretation — 2026-09-29

Joshua’s completed ratings establish these preferences:

- **1:** abstract security, infrastructure, compliance, and contract-policy
  tooling (KeyVault Relay, ClauseGrid, PacketForge, PolicyMesh).
- **2:** narrow back-office financial administration (LedgerLoop).
- **3:** ordinary commercial products with a useful but neutral everyday
  benefit (payroll, returns, landlord operations, building maintenance).
- **4:** clearly helpful consumer, family, health-care, civic, or local-service
  products (veterinary care, staffing coverage, permitting, fleet efficiency,
  elder-care coordination, clinical-trial navigation).
- **5:** direct support for people or community-serving local businesses, such
  as benefits access, accessibility, food access, and independent groceries.
